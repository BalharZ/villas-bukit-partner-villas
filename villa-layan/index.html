About the Villa

Privacy. Silence.
Tropical Living.

Hidden high above Lipah Bay, Villa Layan is a private tropical retreat designed for guests seeking peace, panoramic views and authentic island living.

Surrounded by lush greenery and overlooking the sea, the villa offers complete privacy, spacious open living areas and a calm atmosphere far from crowded tourist areas.

Crafted from natural wood and stone, Villa Layan combines organic architecture with the warmth and comfort of relaxed tropical living.
